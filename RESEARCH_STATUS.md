# Research status and boundaries

## What this checkout demonstrates

The included modules implement source-bound admission, trace histories and
versions, conflicts, dependency invalidation, withdrawal, replay, memory tiers,
gated state-level consolidation, and snapshot/restore. Included fixtures and
tests are synthetic engineering checks. A passing check is evidence for that
specific behavior, not for all natural-language consumers or research claims.

The larger program's factor-neutral lifecycle comparison is synthetic,
fixed-model, oracle-label evidence. It does not establish natural-language
lifecycle detection or superiority of a standalone retriever. Earlier positive
interpretations with information confounds were withdrawn; public-data local
retrieval weaknesses remain part of the record.

The small-model UAI_2 comparisons did not establish structural superiority over
GRUs. A reported approximately 3.4x quantity refers to a specified weighted
governance-cost proxy within a model, not hardware speed or an unconditional
advantage over GRUs. StageB remains `HOLD_DATA_DEFINITION_UNRESOLVED`.

No new benchmark, training run, biological experiment or scientific conclusion
is produced by this delivery. Large-model continual learning, biological
equivalence, reliable unconstrained language interpretation, and autonomous
scientific discovery remain unproven goals. Their private datasets and raw
reports are not included.

## UAI_1 phase-specific updates

The accepted factor-neutral result uses 128 synthetic worlds, shared candidates,
oracle lifecycle labels, a fixed reader, and matched row/context budgets.
Its approximately 17.9–19.1 percentage-point within-surface effects chiefly
come from drift and rollback, with losses on ordinary correction and harm
probes. The independent 66,560-call successor must not be combined with the
formally withdrawn positive interpretation of the earlier 26,624-call study.
