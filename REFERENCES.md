# References

- McClelland, J. L., McNaughton, B. L. and O'Reilly, R. C. (1995).
  *Why there are complementary learning systems in the hippocampus and neocortex.*
  Psychological Review. Background for the separation of rapid acquisition
  and slower integration; no literal biological equivalence is claimed.
- [SYNAPSE public implementation](https://github.com/hq0709/synapse),
  revision `e58e7ffa8dcdf15105b01a2689ff16327182af27`.
  The original competition-module docstring attributes its global operator's
  mathematical shape. The private review copy preserves that attribution and
  the upstream MIT license. This is not an end-to-end reproduction or an
  accepted ranking comparison against the external system.

The larger program also studied public memory benchmarks and small recurrent
controllers. Those datasets, model calls and historical raw reports are not
part of this portable source subset.
